2. Keep the Momentum Going

Once the eBook is live, don’t let it sit. Maintain momentum
through:


  • Reader engagement: Ask for feedback, questions, or tes-

    your book.
  • Free promos: Amazon offers free Kindle promotional
    days—something many indie authors have found to
    increase visibility and spark sustained interest




  • Targeted categories and keywords: Proper placement on
   Amazon can help your book show up next to others readers
    already buy. It’s all about relevance.




3. Spread It Like Love

What if your eBook could spark a movement?

  • Use BookTok—TikTok’s book-loving community—to
    share your message in short, impactful videos.
