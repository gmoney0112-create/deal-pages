   ᒗ Chapter 1: Why Write an eBook?

(And Why God Cares About Your Voice)



Let’s be real. The idea of writing a book sounds about as possible
as buying front row Beyoncé tickets on minimum wage. But hear
me out: you don’t need to be Stephen King, J.K. Rowling, or T.D.
Jakes to write something powerful. You need three things: your
story, your faith, and your willingness to type it out (even if
it’s on your cracked iPhone screen).
  See, your life matters. Your story matters. And the world
is literally waiting to hear what you’ve been through. Not the
“perfect church kid story,” not the Instagram highlight reel—
nah, I’m talking about the real, raw, unfiltered stuff: the nights
you didn’t think you’d make it, the jobs that didn’t call you back,
the relationships that left scars, the prayers you whispered half
believing. That’s your gold. That’s the material God wants to
use.

But Why an eBook?

Because an eBook is the street mixtape of the publishing world.


  • You don’t need a publisher.
  • You don’t need Barnes & Noble.
  • You don’t even need money to print copies.


All you need is WiFi, some hustle, and the courage to hit save as
PDF.


An eBook lets you:


  • Share your testimony in a way that can be downloaded
    instantly worldwide.
  • Start a side hustle (yep, you can sell it for $5, $10, or more).
  • Build your brand—whether that’s as a speaker, en-
    trepreneur, or just someone trying to help others not make
    the same mistakes.




Real Talk: Why God Cares About Your Voice

Let’s get deep for a second. You might be thinking, “Why would
God care about me writing an eBook? Isn’t He busy parting seas
and blessing people with jobs?”
  Here’s why: because your story could be the exact thing
someone else needs to keep going.
  Think about Paul.       Dude was in jail, broke, beaten up,
betrayed—yet he wrote letters. Letters that became scripture.

His writings have outlived empires, presidents, and even
iPhones. That’s the power of writing.
  God isn’t asking you to be perfect. He’s asking you to be
willing. Willing to take the pain, the lessons, the wins, and
put them into words. That’s ministry. That’s purpose. That’s
obedience.




Humor Break

Writing a book sounds intimidating, but let me keep it real with
you:


  • If DJ Khaled can make millions shouting “Another One,”
    you can write 40 pages.
  • If Kanye can release a 27-track album with no tracklist, you
    can structure 12 chapters.
  • If Peter—who denied Jesus three times—still got chosen to
    preach, you can hit publish.


You don’t have to be polished. You just have to be available.




A Challenge for You


Right now, grab a piece of paper (or Notes app if your charger’s
nearby) and write down:


  1. One struggle you’ve overcome.
  2. One lesson you’ve learned.

Closing This Chapter

The first step isn’t about knowing everything. It’s about de-
ciding: “My voice matters. My story matters. And I will not stay
silent.”
  So when you write your eBook, you’re not just chasing clout,
money, or likes—you’re being obedient to the call on your life.
And trust me, the same God who multiplied five loaves and two
fish can multiply your 40 pages into a legacy that outlives you.
