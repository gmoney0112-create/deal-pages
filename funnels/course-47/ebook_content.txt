   ᒗ Chapter 1: Why Write an eBook?

(And Why God Cares About Your Voice)



Let’s be real. The idea of writing a book sounds about as possible
as buying front row Beyoncé tickets on minimum wage. But hear
me out: you don’t need to be Stephen King, J.K. Rowling, or T.D.
Jakes to write something powerful. You need three things: your
story, your faith, and your willingness to type it out (even if
it’s on your cracked iPhone screen).
  See, your life matters. Your story matters. And the world
is literally waiting to hear what you’ve been through. Not the
“perfect church kid story,” not the Instagram highlight reel—
nah, I’m talking about the real, raw, unfiltered stuff: the nights
you didn’t think you’d make it, the jobs that didn’t call you back,
the relationships that left scars, the prayers you whispered half
believing. That’s your gold. That’s the material God wants to
use.
  ↲ Habakkuk 2:2 says, “Write the vision and make it plain.”
  Notice it didn’t say “only if you have a PhD” or “only if you’re
Instagram verified.” Nope. It just said: write.

But Why an eBook?

Because an eBook is the street mixtape of the publishing world.


  • You don’t need a publisher.
  • You don’t need Barnes & Noble.
  • You don’t even need money to print copies.


All you need is WiFi, some hustle, and the courage to hit save as
PDF.


An eBook lets you:


  • Share your testimony in a way that can be downloaded
    instantly worldwide.
  • Start a side hustle (yep, you can sell it for $5, $10, or more).
  • Build your brand—whether that’s as a speaker, en-
    trepreneur, or just someone trying to help others not make
    the same mistakes.




Real Talk: Why God Cares About Your Voice

Let’s get deep for a second. You might be thinking, “Why would
God care about me writing an eBook? Isn’t He busy parting seas
and blessing people with jobs?”
  Here’s why: because your story could be the exact thing
someone else needs to keep going.
  Think about Paul.       Dude was in jail, broke, beaten up,
betrayed—yet he wrote letters. Letters that became scripture.

 ᒗ Chapter 2: Choosing Your Message

       (What’s in Your Spirit to Share)



Alright, fam—so you decided you’re gonna write this eBook.
You’ve got faith, some ideas bouncing around, and probably a
half-empty bag of Hot Cheetos on the table. Now what?
  Before you start typing, you gotta figure out what message
you’re carrying. Think of it like this: God didn’t give Noah
blueprints for a scooter—He gave him the blueprint for an ark,
because an ark was needed. Same with you. Your message isn’t
random; it’s assigned.




Step 1: Look at Your Life

Your message usually hides in three areas:


  1. What you’ve survived. (Broken home? Toxic relationship?
     Being broke but somehow making it through?)
  2. What you’ve learned. (Budgeting, staying positive, trust-
     ing God, starting a hustle.)

  3. What you care about. (Helping others, giving advice,
     sharing faith, teaching skills.)


If you can survive it, learn from it, or care deeply about it—
congratulations, you’ve got an eBook topic.




Step 2: Don’t Overthink

Some of y’all get stuck thinking your eBook has to be “deep”
like a college professor. Stop it. Please. Nobody needs another
300-page manual on tax law (unless you really love that, then
God bless you).
  Instead, think: “What can I share that’ll help somebody tomor-
row?”
  Examples:




  • How to Make $200 a Week with DoorDash Without Losing Your
    Mind.
  • Faith Over Fear: How I Trusted God After a Breakup.
  • Survival Guide for Young Adults Who Are Broke, Busy, and
    Battling Anxiety.


Notice something? They’re short, catchy, and straight to the
point.

ᒗ Chapter 4: Structuring Like a Pro (12

    Chapters, 40 Pages, One Mission)



Okay, so you’ve got your message, you’ve prayed on it, and
you’ve pulled viral inspiration from Beyoncé, Cardi, and Paul.
Now let’s get practical: how do you actually structure a 40-page
eBook without pulling your hair out?
  Spoiler alert: it’s easier than putting IKEA furniture together.




The Mixtape Analogy ₒ

Think of your eBook like a mixtape.


  • Each chapter = a track.
  • The whole book = the album.
  • You don’t need every track to be 20 minutes long; you just
    need it to flow.


Some tracks (chapters) will be hype, some chill, some deep—but
they all work together to tell your story.

HOW TO WRITE A 40-PAGE EBOOK (EVEN IF YOU’VE NEVER WRITTEN A GROCERY
                                LIST)


The Magic Formula

Here’s the secret:


  • 12 chapters.
  • 3–4 pages per chapter.
  • That’s it.


You don’t need to write 300 pages. You don’t need to stress
about word counts. Just aim for 12 clear sections.


↲ 1 Corinthians 14:40 — “Let all things be done decently and in
order.”
  God is a God of order. And your eBook should reflect that.




Break It Down

Here’s a simple structure for any eBook (you can remix it as
needed):


  1. Intro/Why This Book Exists (grab attention)
  2. Your Story/Struggle (show your humanity)
  3. The Lesson Learned (teach something real)
  4. The Faith Factor (scripture or God principle)
  5. Action Steps (give readers practical moves)


Repeat that rhythm for each chapter. It’s like a 5-step dance
routine.

ᒗ Chapter 5: Writing with Flow (How to

So now you’ve got your structure—your 12 chapters are like
neatly stacked Lego blocks. But here comes the hard part:
actually filling those pages with words that don’t sound like
a robot wrote them.
  Relax. You don’t need to be Shakespeare. You just need flow.




Flow = Voice + Clarity + Spirit

Your voice is what makes your writing unique. Some people are
funny, some deep, some motivational. Your job is to write how
you talk—raw, real, and unfiltered (but still respectful, don’t be
wildin’).
  Your clarity comes from being simple. Short sentences hit
harder. Think Twitter, not a law textbook.
  Your Spirit is what makes your words alive. Pray before you
write. Ask God: “Lord, give me the words. Use me as Your
pen.” That’s how you make sure you’re not just writing, but

Practical Writing Hacks

  1. Talk it out. If you can’t write it, voice-record it. Pretend
     you’re telling your friend a story, then transcribe it.
  2. Write ugly first. Don’t try to be perfect. Get words down,
     then edit later.
  3. Use memes/slang sparingly. One or two TikTok refer-
     ences? Funny. Ten per page? Annoying.




  1. Mix story + scripture. Tell what happened, then back it

Here’s the truth: people don’t remember facts, they remember
stories. That’s why Jesus spoke in parables, not PowerPoints.
  If you want your eBook to connect, you’ve got to master
storytelling. And no, that doesn’t mean making stuff up. It
means telling your truth in a way that makes people lean in like
they’re binge-watching Netflix.




Why Storytelling Works

  1. Stories stick. You’ll forget statistics, but you’ll never
     forget “David vs. Goliath.”
  2. Stories connect. When someone reads your struggle, they
     see themselves.
  3. Stories inspire. Your testimony = someone else’s survival
     guide.

The Show vs. Tell Rule

Bad writing tells. Good writing shows.


  • Telling: “I was broke.”
  • Showing: “My card got declined on a $1 McChicken. That
    cashier looked at me like I asked for a loan.”


See how the second one paints a picture? That’s what makes
readers laugh, cry, or nod their heads.




Make It Relatable

Don’t try to sound perfect. Readers don’t connect to perfect—
they connect to messy.
  Examples you can use:


  • Failing a class.
  • Arguing with parents.
  • Struggling with self-esteem.
  • Praying for a breakthrough and thinking, “God, You still
    there?”


Be honest. Vulnerability is powerful.

  Your eBook is the same. You need to polish it—without
stripping away your personality.




Why Editing Matters

  1. Respect for your reader. If your book is full of typos, people
     won’t take your wisdom seriously.
  2. Clarity. Editing makes your message clear.
  3. Confidence. A polished book makes you proud to share it.
