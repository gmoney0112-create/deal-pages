# Sales Funnel Design Style Guide

## Design Philosophy

### Color Palette
**Primary Teal Green**: #0D9488 (Tailwind teal-600)
- Used for: Primary CTAs, headers, accent elements
- Psychology: Growth, prosperity, trust, fresh starts

**Supporting Colors**:
- **Deep Charcoal**: #1F2937 (text, contrast)
- **Warm White**: #FEFEFE (backgrounds)
- **Soft Gray**: #F3F4F6 (section backgrounds)
- **Success Green**: #10B981 (confirmations, testimonials)
- **Warning Amber**: #F59E0B (urgency elements)

### Typography
**Display Font**: "Playfair Display" (serif, elegant, trustworthy)
- Used for: Main headings, hero titles, section headers
- Weights: 700 (bold), 600 (semibold)

**Body Font**: "Inter" (sans-serif, clean, readable)
- Used for: Body text, buttons, navigation
- Weights: 400 (regular), 500 (medium), 600 (semibold)

### Visual Language
**Authentic & Relatable**: No overly polished corporate aesthetics
**Faith-Forward**: Subtle biblical references without being preachy
**Trust-Building**: Testimonials, guarantees, social proof prominently featured
**Action-Oriented**: Clear CTAs with strong contrast and hover effects

## Visual Effects & Styling

### Used Libraries
1. **Anime.js** - Smooth animations for testimonials, CTAs, and page transitions
2. **Typed.js** - Typewriter effect for hero headline
3. **Splide.js** - Testimonial carousel with smooth transitions
4. **ECharts.js** - Success statistics visualization
5. **p5.js** - Subtle particle background effect in hero section
6. **Splitting.js** - Text reveal animations for section headers

### Animation & Effects
**Hero Section**:
- Typewriter animation for main headline
- Subtle floating particles background (p5.js)
- CTA button with pulse animation on hover

**Testimonials**:
- Auto-rotating carousel with fade transitions
- Manual navigation arrows
- 5-second auto-rotation with pause on hover

**Course Curriculum**:
- Accordion-style expandable sections
- Smooth slide animations for content reveal
- Progress indicators showing course structure

**Scroll Animations**:
- Elements fade in as they enter viewport (top 50%)
- Staggered animations for feature lists
- Parallax effect on background elements (±8% translateY)

### Header Effect
**Gradient Text Animation**: Main headline cycles through teal shades
**Background**: Subtle particle system with floating dots in teal
**CTA Button**: 3D lift effect with shadow expansion on hover

### Hover Effects
**Buttons**: 
- Teal background with white text
- Scale transform (1.05x) with shadow
- Smooth 200ms transition

**Testimonial Cards**:
- Subtle lift with shadow expansion
- Scale transform (1.02x)
- Border glow effect in teal

**Order Bump Toggles**:
- Smooth color transition when selected
- Checkmark animation on selection
- Price update with highlight animation

### Mobile Responsiveness
- Touch-optimized interactions
- Swipe gestures for testimonial carousel
- Collapsible navigation menu
- Large touch targets (minimum 44px)

### Accessibility
- High contrast ratios (4.5:1 minimum)
- Focus indicators for keyboard navigation
- Screen reader friendly markup
- Alt text for all images

## Psychological Triggers Used

### Social Proof
- 6 detailed testimonials with photos
- Success statistics with animated counters
- "Join 2,847+ writers" social proof number

### Urgency & Scarcity
- Limited-time bonus offers
- "Special launch pricing" messaging
- Countdown timer for bonuses

### Authority
- Author credentials and background
- Media mentions and endorsements
- "Featured in" publication logos

### Risk Reversal
- 30-day money-back guarantee
- "No questions asked" refund policy
- Faith-based guarantee messaging

### Community
- Skool community invitation
- "You're not alone" messaging
- Peer success stories

This design creates a trustworthy, authentic experience that resonates with the target audience while maximizing conversion through proven psychological triggers and smooth interactions.
